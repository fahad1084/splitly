This Privacy Policy explains how Splitly ("we", "our", "the app") collects, uses, and protects your information when you use the Splitly mobile application. Splitly is an expense-splitting app that helps you share and track group expenses with friends, family, and roommates.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Email address — required to create an account and sign in (via email/password or Google Sign-In).
  • Full name — shown to other members in your groups.
  • Phone number — optional, only if you choose to add it to your profile.
  • Profile avatar color — a cosmetic preference stored with your profile.

Financial & Expense Data

  • Expenses you create: title, amount, currency, category, and notes.
  • Receipt photos you scan or upload (processed on-device for text recognition; the image itself may be stored so you can view it later).
  • Group details: group name, description, currency, and members.
  • Settlement records (who paid whom, and how much).

Communication Data

  • Chat messages you send within a group, including text, photos, and voice messages.

Device & Technical Data

  • A push-notification device token (used only to deliver notifications to your device — see Section 3).
  • Standard app diagnostic information (e.g. crash logs) that may be collected by our infrastructure providers.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To create and manage your account.
  • To let you create groups, add expenses, and split costs with others.
  • To calculate balances and simplify who-owes-who between group members.
  • To enable real-time group chat.
  • To send you push notifications about activity relevant to you.
  • To generate spending reports and exports (PDF/CSV) that you request.

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal information. We share data only with the service providers that power the app:

  • Supabase — our backend provider, which hosts our database, authentication, file storage, and real-time messaging infrastructure.
  • Google Firebase Cloud Messaging — used solely to deliver push notifications to your device.
  • Google Sign-In — if you choose to sign in with Google, Google shares your name and email with us to create your account.

Receipt-scanning text recognition runs on-device using Google's ML Kit and does not send your receipt images to Google's servers for that purpose.

4. Shared Group Data & Account Deletion

Splitly is a shared ledger — when you add an expense to a group, the details are visible to the other members of that group, because they need accurate records to know what they owe or are owed. This is core to how the app works, similar to other expense-splitting apps.

If you delete your account from within the app (Profile → Delete Account):

  • Your login is permanently disabled — you can never sign back into that account.
  • Your personal details (name, phone number, device token) are removed and your name is replaced with "Deleted User".
  • Expenses and settlements you were part of in shared groups remain on record (attributed to "Deleted User") so that other members' balances stay accurate — the same way it would if you removed yourself as a member from an ongoing shared expense sheet.

If you'd like your data fully removed from a specific group, you can leave that group before deleting your account, or contact us using the details in Section 8.

5. Data Security

Your data is transmitted using encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS) and stored with our backend provider, Supabase, which enforces row-level security so that only members of a group can access that group's data. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we work to protect your information using industry-standard practices.

6. Data Retention

We retain your account and group data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we retain only the anonymized records described in Section 4, for as long as needed to preserve the integrity of shared group ledgers.

7. Children's Privacy

Splitly is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.
